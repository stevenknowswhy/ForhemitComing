Plain-English Summary — Not Legal Terms
What this document does, in one paragraph

This letter hires Forhemit to run your ESOP transition — the 120-day process of selling your company to an employee ownership trust. Forhemit is not the buyer; the employee ownership trust is. Forhemit coordinates the professional team (trustee, ERISA attorney, appraiser, lender, QofE provider), manages the timeline, and after closing, stays on for 12–24 months to help the business run smoothly under employee ownership.

The company pays Forhemit's fees as a normal business expense — not you personally, and not out of your sale proceeds. Forhemit takes no equity, no board seat, and does not appear on your closing statement. Total fees are fixed based on your company's EBITDA tier (see §04). If the deal does not close, fee obligations after the Retainer stop at whichever milestone gate was last cleared.

Your attorney and CPA should focus on §04 (fees and timing), §05 (SBA independence), and §08–§11 (legal terms). Wiring instructions are on the last page.

§ 01Parties and Nature of Engagement

This Engagement Letter ("Agreement") is entered into between Forhemit Stewardship Management Co., a California Public Benefit Corporation, doing business as Forhemit Transition Stewardship ("Forhemit"), and [Company] ("Company"), acting through [Authorized Officer].

Forhemit is a transaction stewardship firm that facilitates employee ownership transitions — coordinating the transaction team, managing the process timeline, preparing the lender and trustee package, and providing post-close operational stewardship for a period of 12 to 24 months following closing.

IMPORTANT: Forhemit is not a buyer, investor, lender, broker, attorney, or financial advisor. The ESOP trust is the legal acquirer. All Forhemit fees are paid by the Company as ordinary operating business expenses.

ERISA DISCLAIMER: Forhemit is NOT an ERISA fiduciary. All ERISA matters must be reviewed by independent ERISA counsel engaged and directed solely by the ESOP trustee.

TAX DISCLAIMER: Forhemit does NOT provide tax advice. All tax matters, including §1042 election analysis, must be reviewed by independent tax professionals.
§ 02Scope of Services

Forhemit's engagement encompasses the following services across a structured 120-day transaction process, organized into five phases with four Hard Stop gates:

01Transaction Coordination — Assembly and management of the full professional team including trustee, ERISA counsel, appraiser, QofE provider, and lender.
02Lender Package Preparation — Compilation of financial, operational, and legal documentation required for lender 7(a) underwriting and trustee review.
03COOP Development — Continuity of Operations Plan across four progressive versions (v1–v4) from Day 11 through Day 90, culminating in trustee sign-off at Gate 4.
04Process Management — Timeline governance including four Hard Stop gates at Days 11, 35, 63, and 90.
05Post-Close Stewardship — Optional operational support for 12–24 months beginning Day 121, at the Annual Stewardship Fee set forth in §04.

Services do not include legal representation, tax advice, ERISA fiduciary services, brokerage, investment advice, or capital provision of any kind.

The Retainer is earned in full upon execution of this Agreement. It does not apply toward the Transition Success Fee or any other fee obligation. The Retainer is fully refundable for a period of 30 calendar days following execution ("Refund Period"), provided Company delivers written notice to deals@forhemit.com prior to expiration. Following expiration, the Retainer is earned and non-refundable.

Refundable Period Active: Company may terminate without cause and receive a full refund if written notice is delivered within the Refund Period.

Tax Treatment Note (for Company CPA). Forhemit's fees are structured as ordinary and necessary business expenses deductible under IRC §162 in the year incurred, not as capitalizable transaction costs under IRC §263. This treatment is consistent with Forhemit's role as a service provider (not a broker, finder, or transaction participant) and the fact that Forhemit's compensation is not contingent on the sale price or proceeds. The Company's tax advisor should confirm this treatment in light of the Company's specific facts and recent guidance.
Milestone Gate Clearance — CPA Reference. This table uses the word "Milestone" for payment triggers and the word "Gate" for process checkpoints. They are related but not identical. The four Hard Stop Gates (Days 11, 35, 63, 90 under §02) must clear before their corresponding Milestones (Days 1, 45, 60, 120) are billable. If a Gate does not clear, the corresponding Milestone is not owed — the engagement pauses at the last cleared Gate. This means the maximum cash exposure for a deal that fails at any point is the sum of milestones already invoiced at that point, not the Total Engagement Fee.
Late Payment: Fees not paid within 10 business days accrue interest at 1.5% per month.
Broker Commission: Forhemit does not adjust, share, defer, or appear as a line item affecting the broker's commission.

Tail Provision. If, within 180 days following termination of this Agreement (the "Tail Period"), the Company closes an ESOP transaction with a professional counterparty first introduced to Company by Forhemit during the engagement (each, a "Qualifying Counterparty"), then the Transition Success Fee specified in §04 shall be owed in full.

For purposes of this provision, a "Qualifying Counterparty" means a trustee, ERISA counsel, appraiser, lender, QofE provider, or other professional advisor with whom Forhemit had substantive documented contact during the engagement — as evidenced by written communication (email, letter, or meeting notes) recorded in Forhemit's engagement files and disclosed to Company in a termination-date "Introductions Schedule" delivered within ten (10) business days of termination.

The Tail Provision does not apply to: (a) counterparties with whom the Company had a documented pre-existing relationship prior to the Effective Date; (b) counterparties the Company identified and introduced to Forhemit (not the reverse); or (c) any termination resulting from Forhemit's material breach.

The Tail Period is fixed at 180 days from the termination effective date and is not renewable or extendable.

§ 10Limitation of Liability

Mutual Cap. Except as provided below, the total aggregate liability of either Party to the other arising out of or related to this Agreement — whether in contract, tort, warranty, or otherwise — shall not exceed the total fees actually paid by Company to Forhemit under this Agreement as of the date the claim accrues.

Carve-Outs. The liability cap in this §10 does not apply to: (a) a Party's willful misconduct or gross negligence; (b) a Party's fraud or intentional misrepresentation; (c) breach of confidentiality obligations under §06; (d) indemnification obligations under §11 for third-party claims; or (e) a Party's unpaid fees, invoices, or other monetary obligations under this Agreement.

Each Party (the "Indemnifying Party") shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other Party and its officers, directors, employees, and affiliates (collectively, the "Indemnified Party") from and against any third-party claims, damages, losses, and reasonable attorneys' fees arising from the Indemnifying Party's: (a) gross negligence or willful misconduct; (b) material breach of this Agreement; or (c) violation of applicable law in connection with its performance hereunder.

Additional Company Indemnification. In addition to the mutual obligations above, Company shall indemnify Forhemit against third-party claims arising from: (a) Company's misrepresentation or omission of material facts in information provided to Forhemit, the trustee, lender, appraiser, or QofE provider; (b) Company's fraud; or (c) Company's infringement of third-party intellectual property rights in materials provided to Forhemit. This asymmetric additional indemnification reflects Forhemit's reliance on Company-supplied data throughout the transaction and is narrowly tailored to claims arising from data Forhemit cannot independently verify.

Procedure. The Indemnified Party shall (i) promptly notify the Indemnifying Party of any claim; (ii) grant the Indemnifying Party sole control of the defense and settlement, provided no settlement imposing non-monetary obligations on the Indemnified Party may be made without its written consent; and (iii) reasonably cooperate in the defense at the Indemnifying Party's expense.
