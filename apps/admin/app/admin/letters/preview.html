RE: An Introduction to Forhemit Transition Stewardship
Your Story

Beyond the Purchase Price.

Most business owners who reach this stage have spent years — sometimes decades — building something that cannot be fully captured on a spreadsheet.

"The financial results are there, but they tell only part of the story."

The team you kept together through difficult years, the reputation you built one relationship at a time, and the decisions you made when no one was watching are all part of the value you’ve created. They deserve to be treated that way.

What We Do

A private-market version of an IPO — without the unknown buyer.

We help facilitate and transition a private market IPO (initial public offering) to your employees.

"You receive full fair market value (FMV) for your business."

You receive the financial benefits of a sale, but instead of transferring ownership to an unknown outside buyer, ownership passes to the employees who helped build the company. Leadership remains in place, the culture stays intact, and the business continues serving the customers and communities that helped make it successful.

How We Work 120 Day structured timeline

We operate on a structured timeline designed to create clarity and momentum without disrupting the day-to-day operation of your business.

01
Valuation Independent FMV appraisal
02
Financing Capital stack assembly
03
Design Transaction structure
04
Close Implementation & transfer

Throughout the engagement, you will have access to a live project dashboard that shows exactly where we are at any given time. We meet regularly to review progress, discuss upcoming milestones, and answer questions as they arise.

Our job is to manage the complexity so that you can remain focused on your business, your people, and your future.

Why It Matters

Legacy, people, and continuity — not just the price.

A business transition is rarely just a financial decision. It is a decision about people, legacy, and what happens to something you spent years building.

  • Employees who helped build the company benefit directly from its success
  • Leadership, culture, and customer relationships stay intact
  • Communities that depend on the business are protected
  • Your legacy is preserved — not absorbed into a portfolio
We appreciate the opportunity to introduce ourselves and would welcome a conversation to learn more about your goals and explore whether this approach may be a good fit for you and your company.

RE: Initial Review and Alignment for Acme Manufacturing, Inc.
Our Role

We are not the buyer. We are the facilitator.

This letter is meant to do one thing: give you a clear, practical picture of where we believe Acme Manufacturing, Inc. stands today and how a Forhemit engagement would work.

"Forhemit is not acting as the buyer of your business — our role is to facilitate a fair, credible, and defensible purchase price and employee stock offering."

We often describe the structure as a private IPO: ownership moves to the employees who helped build the company, while leadership, culture, and operations remain steady. Our incentives are tied to the success of the transition, not to moving the final price up or down.

Initial Valuation $20M Preliminary estimate

Final price set by independent FMV appraisal.

After weeks of discussing your company with Martin & Associates and conducting our own internal review, we anticipate a preliminary starting valuation of approximately $20 million.

  • This is not a formal valuation and not the final purchase price
  • The official price is set by an independent, third-party FMV appraisal
  • You review the FMV determination before deciding whether to proceed
Fee Structure

Milestone-tied. We only earn when you reach defined checkpoints.

We believe our compensation should be tied to meaningful progress and successful outcomes. After an initial retainer, remaining fees are tied directly to specific milestones:

  1. 1When you approve the Fair Market Value determination letter
  2. 2When we secure a lender loan commitment acceptable to you
  3. 3When the transaction successfully closes

This structure ensures that we are working toward the same goal: a successful transition that works for you, the company, and the employees who will carry it forward.

How We Are Paid

Company pays as a business expense — not deducted from your proceeds.

Because Forhemit is providing transition and stewardship services, our fees are paid by the company as a business expense, subject to review by your tax and legal advisors.

  1. 1Our fees are not deducted from the amount you receive at closing
  2. 2Forhemit is not the buyer and is not reducing your purchase price
  3. 3You retain the ability to consider outside offers while building a credible employee-ownership path — real optionality, not a closed process
We would welcome the opportunity to review this framework with you and answer any questions. If it feels aligned with your goals, the next step would be a brief call to discuss timing, priorities, and what would be needed to move forward.
